About

I was born in Sendai, Japan and grew up in Shanghai, China, then moved to Vancouver for high school. University took me east — an undergraduate degree in Toronto and a master’s in Montréal — before I came back to Vancouver for medicine.

Away from the clinic

Underwater

Scuba diving

Certified through PADI Advanced Open Water. Koh Tao, Thailand is my favourite place to dive, and where most of the photographs below were taken.

Diving has made me detail oriented and observant of the small things. You check your gear, then you check it again, and you learn to notice what is not quite right before it becomes something larger.
