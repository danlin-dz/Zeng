Research experience

Ophthalmology

  • Feb 2026 – present
    Acute Angle-Closure Glaucoma (ACG) & Non-Ophthalmic Anticholinergics

    Supervisors: Dr. Vincent Tony Wong, Dr. Rob Pintwala

    Led a sex-stratified disproportionality analysis of ACG reporting in patients on non-ophthalmic anticholinergics; independently learned and applied R to conduct the sensitivity analyzes.

  • Feb 2026 – present
    Intraocular Hemorrhage & Antiplatelet Use

    Supervisors: Dr. Vincent Tony Wong, Dr. Rob Pintwala

    Characterized the association between antiplatelet use and multiple intraocular hemorrhage subtypes.

    Integrated the findings with existing evidence to clarify clinically relevant ocular bleeding risk.

  • Sept 2025 – Jul 2026
    Taxane Chemotherapy-Associated Ocular Adverse Events

    Supervisors: Dr. Rob Pintwala, Dr. Ahmed Al-Ghoul

    Spearheaded a characterization of taxane-associated ocular adverse events.

    Identified gaps in the evidence and critically evaluated sources of bias and study limitations in a structured literature review, to support rigorous interpretation of the findings.

  • May 2026 – Jul 2026
    Ophthalmic Surgical Wait Times

    Supervisor: Dr. Eduardo Navajas

    Worked with other medical students to calculate regional trends in ophthalmic surgical wait times across British Columbia health authorities, comparing surgical backlog against ophthalmologist supply.

    Characterized and emphasized the disparities in access experienced by Indigenous communities.

Oncology care

  • Jan 2026 – Jul 2026
    Social Isolation & Cancer Survival

    Supervisor: Dr. John-Jose Nunez

    A recent cancer diagnosis in my family is what moved me to understand how psychosocial factors shape cancer outcomes.

    Led the analysis of a population cohort of 45,651 patients; living alone, lacking regular social contact, and elevated depressive or anxiety symptoms each significantly increased mortality risk.

  • Sept 2025 – Jun 2026
    Global Radiotherapy Utilization

    Supervisor: Dr. Jessica Chan

    Screened studies for a systematic review comparing evidence-based radiotherapy need against real-world utilization across health systems, to characterise global inequities in access.

  • Nov 2024 – Nov 2025
    Methadone for Breakthrough Neuropathic Cancer Pain

    Supervisor: Dr. Mathieos Belayneh

    Worked with physicians to review 706 patient charts; identified methadone as the second-most common breakthrough analgesic in British Columbia, with no documented severe adverse events.

  • Apr 2024 – Jan 2026
    Rectal Squamous Cell Carcinoma (rSCC) in British Columbia

    Supervisor: Dr. Jeffrey Sulpher

    Worked with another medical student to standardise data extraction, resolve chart-review discrepancies, and analyze rSCC treatment patterns and outcomes.

  • Dec 2025 – Jun 2026
    Chemoprevention Strategies in Transplant Recipients

    Supervisor: Dr. Fatemeh Jafarian

    Coordinated screening and data extraction across a medical student team, resolving discrepancies and synthesizing evidence on strategies associated with reduced cutaneous malignancy risk in solid organ transplant recipients.

Surgery & global health

  • Feb 2024 – Apr 2026
    Surgical Technique & Laparoscopic Right Hemicolectomy Outcomes

    Supervisor: Dr. Sharadh Sampath

    Led a standardized review of 203 patient charts to examine how anastomotic technique and specimen-extraction site relate to short-term postoperative ileus and long-term incisional hernia.

  • Apr 2026 – Jul 2026
